# Interview Questions for Senior Frontend Engineer

## Technical & Hard Skills
1.  **Modern React Ecosystem**: "Can you walk me through your experience with Next.js and React Server Components? How do you decide when to use Server Components versus Client Components?"
2.  **State Management**: "Tell me about a complex state management problem you solved. What approach did you use (e.g., Redux, Zustand, Context API), and why did you choose it over alternatives?"
3.  **Performance Optimization**: "If a React application you built is suffering from slow load times and janky scrolling, what specific steps would you take to diagnose and fix the performance bottlenecks?"
4.  **API Integration**: "Describe your experience integrating complex, asynchronous APIs (such as those returning streamed data from an AI model). How do you handle loading states, error handling, and optimistic UI updates?"
5.  **Architecture**: "How do you approach architecting a scalable frontend codebase from scratch? What directory structure and foundational tools do you typically put in place?"

## Behavioral & Cultural Fit
1.  **Mentorship**: "Tell me about a time you mentored a junior engineer. How did you identify their areas for growth, and what was the outcome of your mentorship?"
2.  **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: "Describe a situation where you had a disagreement with a designer or product manager regarding a feature's implementation. How did you resolve it?"
3.  **Adaptability (Startup Fit)**: "In an early-stage startup, priorities can shift rapidly. Tell me about a time you had to pivot quickly on a project. How did you manage the change in direction?"
4.  **Initiative**: "Can you share an example of a time you identified a problem in the frontend infrastructure or processes and took the initiative to fix it without being asked?"

## Scenario-Based Problem Solving
1.  **System Design (UI)**: "We want to build a real-time collaborative dashboard where users can interact with an AI model and see live updates. How would you design the frontend architecture for this? What technologies and patterns would you use to ensure real-time synchronization and a smooth user experience?"
2.  **Debugging**: "A user reports that a specific interactive chart on our platform occasionally crashes their browser, but we can't reproduce it locally. How would you go about investigating and resolving this issue in a production environment?"
