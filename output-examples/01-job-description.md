# Senior Frontend Engineer

## About the Role
As a Senior Frontend Engineer at our fast-paced AI startup, you will play a critical role in shaping the user experience of our cutting-edge AI products. You will be responsible for architecting and building highly responsive, intuitive, and performant web interfaces that allow our users to interact seamlessly with complex AI models. You will work closely with our design, product, and machine learning teams to bring innovative features from conception to production.

## Key Responsibilities
*   Lead the development of complex, interactive frontend features using modern web technologies.
*   Architect robust, scalable, and maintainable frontend codebases.
*   Collaborate with designers to translate wireframes and high-fidelity mockups into pixel-perfect UIs.
*   Optimize web applications for maximum speed, scalability, and accessibility.
*   Mentor junior engineers and contribute to establishing frontend best practices across the engineering team.
*   Work closely with backend and ML engineers to integrate APIs and ensure seamless data flow.
*   Participate in code reviews to maintain high code quality standards.

## Required Qualifications
*   5+ years of professional experience in frontend web development.
*   Expert-level proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
*   Deep experience with modern React (Hooks, Context, Server Components) and ecosystem tools (Next.js, Redux/Zustand).
*   Strong understanding of web performance optimization techniques and browser rendering behavior.
*   Experience building responsive, mobile-first web applications.
*   Excellent communication skills and the ability to work effectively in a cross-functional team environment.

## Preferred Qualifications
*   Experience working in an early-stage startup environment.
*   Familiarity with data visualization libraries (e.g., D3.js, Chart.js, Recharts).
*   Previous experience working on products that integrate with AI/ML APIs.
*   Knowledge of WebGL or advanced animation libraries (Framer Motion, Three.js).

## What We Offer
*   Competitive salary and meaningful early-stage equity.
*   Flexible working hours and remote-friendly culture.
*   Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance.
*   Unlimited PTO policy.
*   Annual learning and development stipend.
*   The opportunity to work on bleeding-edge AI technology with a passionate team.
