# Senior Frontend Engineer

## About the Role
As a Senior Frontend Engineer at our fast-paced AI startup, you will take ownership of our core user interfaces, translating complex AI capabilities into intuitive, responsive, and seamless experiences. You will collaborate closely with product managers, designers, and machine learning engineers to build robust web applications from the ground up, shaping how our users interact with cutting-edge AI.

## Key Responsibilities
* Lead the architecture, design, and development of highly scalable frontend web applications.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define requirements, design intuitive UIs, and ship features rapidly.
* Optimize applications for maximum speed, performance, and cross-browser compatibility.
* Build reusable, accessible components and maintain a cohesive design system.
* Mentor junior developers and establish frontend engineering best practices.
* Integrate complex backend and AI model APIs to provide real-time user feedback.
* Participate in code reviews to ensure maintainability and high code quality.

## Required Qualifications
* 5+ years of professional experience building complex web applications.
* Expert-level knowledge of JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
* Extensive experience with React and its modern ecosystem (Next.js, Hooks, Context, State Management).
* Deep understanding of web performance profiling and optimization techniques.
* Proven track record of building accessible and responsive mobile-first interfaces.
* Strong communication skills with an ability to articulate technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders.

## Preferred Qualifications
* Experience working in high-growth or early-stage startups.
* Previous experience integrating with LLMs or machine learning APIs.
* Familiarity with data visualization libraries (e.g., D3, Chart.js) or WebGL.
* Experience with CI/CD pipelines and automated frontend testing frameworks (Cypress, Jest).

## What We Offer
* Competitive salary and meaningful early-stage startup equity.
* Flexible work hours and a remote-first culture.
* Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance.
* Unlimited PTO to ensure you rest and recharge.
* Generous annual stipend for learning, development, and home office setup.
